## Limits
Site support depends on available extractors and accessible non-DRM media; private/login-only, protected and split-only formats are not guaranteed. This implementation selects combined audio/video and does not mux separate tracks.
Speech has chunk/inference latency and model-dependent accuracy/language coverage. Capturable Web audio needs Android 10+ and system consent.
Lettering reconstruction approximates available fonts and simple backgrounds; complex artwork, OCR errors and semantic translation quality need representative raw chapter testing. Supplied screenshots show already overlaid translations and cannot establish clean-source chapter accuracy.
